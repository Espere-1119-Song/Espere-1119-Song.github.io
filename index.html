Research Overview

My research centers on multimodal models and agents, with key areas of focus including:

  • Efficient Long-Sequence Modeling, scaling models to long and streaming inputs such as video with sparse attention, memory, token compression, and recurrent architectures.
  • Real-Time Interactive Agents, building systems that perceive continuous streams and decide when and how to respond.
    • Deciding when to speak and when to stay silent as a video unfolds InteractionBench
    • Hierarchical agentic reasoning that builds a scene step by step from one image HARMONY
  • Benchmarking and Evaluation, building benchmarks and evaluation toolkits that pose challenging real-world tasks, probe the boundaries of model capabilities, and provide insights for future improvement.

Enxin Song, Suhao Yu, Yifei Xu, Barbara Su, Weili Xu, Wenhao Chai, Yao Tang, Jie Deng, Haiyang Xu, Jiatao Gu
arXiv 2026
Paper · Website · Benchmark · Code

Video-Index: A Curated Meta-Benchmark for Video Understanding
Enxin Song, Yinuo Xu, Shusheng Yang, Wenhao Chai, Jiatao Gu
arXiv 2026
Paper · Blog · Benchmark · Code

Video-Index audits 115 video benchmarks with shortcut attacks and curates the 840 hardest verified questions into a single meta-benchmark.

Shufan Sun, Chen Wang, Enxin Song, Jiatao Gu, Lingjie Liu

Paper

HARMONY reconstructs complete 3D indoor scenes from a single image by combining hierarchical agentic reasoning with geometry-based refinement.

VideoNSA: Native Sparse Attention Scales Video Understanding

InteractionBench: A Real-Time Interaction Benchmark for Streaming Video Systems

Demo

Examples

Three videos, two tasks each. Play a video or click a moment: the highlighted column shows what each task must do at that moment, speak or stay silent.

Scoring

An illustrative Alert item with two events. Click the timeline to add or remove a reply, or start from a pattern. Content accuracy grades what the reply says, timing accuracy when it arrives, and silence compliance penalizes premature and repeated replies.

Leaderboard

34 system configurations and two references on 1,060 interactions over 812 videos. Scores range from 0 to 100, and higher is better. Overall = ½ [Acc + H(TA, SC)], with H the harmonic mean. Click a column to sort, and a row for its scores on each task.

Example videos via Wikimedia Commons, trimmed and re-encoded without sound: NSF Diego Garcia hosts basketball game, U.S. Navy (public domain); Boiling water, close up, Sertion (CC BY-SA 1.0); Patata tortilla, Elene B (CC BY-SA 4.0). The example annotations are illustrations, not benchmark items.
